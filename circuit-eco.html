Explore Bihar's Natural Beauty

The Eco Circuit takes you through Bihar's wildlife sanctuaries and riverine landscapes.

Bihar's only tiger reserve, in the Himalayan foothills of West Champaran.

Mauryan rock-cut caves in the Barabar Hills, listed on the Eco Circuit.
