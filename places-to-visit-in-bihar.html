Places to Visit in Bihar & Places to Travel in Bihar

Plan your Bihar trip with UNESCO heritage sites, pilgrimage circuits, hill forts, tiger reserves, and Patna landmarks — the essential places to travel in Bihar.

  1. Home
  2. Places to Visit in Bihar

Bihar is one of India's oldest cultural regions — home to the Buddha's enlightenment, the ruins of Nalanda, Mauryan rock-cut caves, and living pilgrimage traditions along the Ganga. Whether you search for places to visit in Bihar or places to travel in Bihar, this guide groups the state's must-see destinations by theme so you can build a practical itinerary.

Best Places to Visit in Bihar

These are the top tourist places in Bihar that appear on most travel lists — from world-famous Buddhist sites to Patna city icons and hidden hill forts.

Mahabodhi Temple, Bodh Gaya

UNESCO World Heritage site where the Buddha attained enlightenment — the most visited place to travel in Bihar.

Nalanda Mahavihara

UNESCO-listed ruins of the ancient university that drew scholars from across Asia.

Vishnupad Temple, Gaya

Major Hindu pilgrimage site on the Phalgu River, often paired with Bodh Gaya.

Kesariya Stupa

One of the largest Buddhist stupas in the world, in East Champaran.

Takhat Sri Harmandir Ji (Patna Sahib)

One of the five Takhts of Sikhism and the birthplace of Guru Gobind Singh.

Golghar, Patna

Iconic beehive-shaped granary with panoramic views of Patna and the Ganga.

Barabar Caves

India's oldest surviving rock-cut caves with Mauryan polish and Ashokan inscriptions.

Rohtasgarh Fort

Vast hill fort with gates, palaces, and forested ramparts in Rohtas district.

Valmiki Tiger Reserve

Bihar's only tiger reserve with sal forests and Himalayan foothill wildlife.

Ruins of Vikramshila

Pala-era Buddhist university remains near Bhagalpur on the Ganga.

Buddha Smriti Park, Patna

Memorial park with a central stupa and relic chamber in the capital.

Mundeshwari Devi Temple

Among India's oldest continuously worshipped hill temples in Kaimur.

Places to Travel in Bihar by Circuit

Group nearby destinations into themed routes — the easiest way to travel in Bihar without backtracking.

  • Buddhist Circuit — Bodh Gaya, Nalanda, Kesariya, Vikramshila, and Buddha Smriti Park. The core pilgrimage route for places to visit in Bihar.
  • Ramayan Circuit — Gaya, Sitamarhi, and Darbhanga sites linked to the Ramayana.
  • Sikh Circuit — Patna Sahib and gurdwaras tied to Guru Gobind Singh.
  • Eco Circuit — Valmiki Tiger Reserve, Barabar Hills, and river country near Vikramshila.

How to Plan Travel in Bihar

Most travellers fly or take a train to Patna, then use road connections to Bodh Gaya (about 2 hours), Nalanda (about 1.5 hours from Patna), and Gaya. A typical 5–7 day Bihar tourism plan covers Patna landmarks (Golghar, Patna Sahib, Buddha Smriti Park), Bodh Gaya and Rajgir, and Nalanda. Add Rohtasgarh Fort or Valmiki Tiger Reserve if you have extra days.

The best time to visit Bihar is October to March, when temperatures are comfortable for walking ruins and attending festivals. Pair sightseeing with famous Bihar food — especially litti chokha in Patna.

More Places to Travel in Bihar

Beyond our featured guides, travellers often add Rajgir (Vulture's Peak and hot springs), Vaishali (Ashokan pillar), Sitamarhi (Ramayan sites), and Sonepur (famous cattle fair). These pair well with the circuits above.

Bihar Travel FAQs

Common questions about places to visit in Bihar and places to travel in Bihar

What are the best places to visit in Bihar?

Start with Mahabodhi Temple in Bodh Gaya, Nalanda Mahavihara, Barabar Caves, Rohtasgarh Fort, Valmiki Tiger Reserve, Patna Sahib, Golghar, and Kesariya Stupa. See the full list above with links to each destination guide.

What are the best places to travel in Bihar?

Plan themed routes: the Buddhist Circuit for pilgrimage heritage, Patna for city landmarks, the Ramayan and Sikh circuits for spiritual travel, and the Eco Circuit for wildlife and nature. Most itineraries begin in Patna or Bodh Gaya.

How many days do you need to travel in Bihar?

Five to seven days covers Bodh Gaya, Nalanda, Rajgir, and Patna. Add two to three more days for Rohtasgarh Fort, Valmiki Tiger Reserve, or Vikramshila for a fuller Bihar tourism experience.

What is the best time to visit Bihar?

October to March offers the most comfortable weather for visiting ruins, forts, and wildlife reserves. Winter is also when festivals like Chhath Puja draw visitors to Bihar's rivers.
