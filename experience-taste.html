Taste

Everyday plates and festival sweets: litti chokha, Maner laddoo, Buxar soan papdi, and GI produce.

Litti chokha

Roasted wheat balls with mashed spiced vegetables — Bihar's best-known savoury plate, easy to find in Patna.

Langar at Patna Sahib

Simple vegetarian meals at Takhat Sri Harmandir Ji, open to every visitor.

Soan papdi

A flaky gram-flour sweet associated with Buxar and Munger.

Maner laddoo & GI foods

Maner's famous laddoo, plus mithila makhana, Shahi litchi, and Silao khaja.
