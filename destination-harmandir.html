About Takhat Sri Harmandir Ji

Takhat Sri Harmandir Ji, Patna Sahib is one of the most important Sikh pilgrimage sites. It marks the birthplace of Guru Gobind Singh, the tenth Sikh Guru. This sacred gurdwara holds immense religious significance for Sikhs worldwide.

The gurdwara was built by Maharaja Ranjit Singh in the 19th century on the site where Guru Gobind Singh was born in 1666. It is one of the five Takhts (thrones) of Sikhism, making it a supreme authority in Sikh religious matters.

Religious Significance

As one of the five Takhts of Sikhism, Patna Sahib holds supreme religious importance. It is the birthplace of Guru Gobind Singh, who founded the Khalsa Panth. The gurdwara houses many sacred relics and manuscripts related to the Guru's life.

Architecture

The gurdwara features beautiful Sikh architecture with a large marble courtyard and golden domes. The main prayer hall houses the Guru Granth Sahib. The complex includes various buildings, a museum, and accommodation for pilgrims.

Visitor Information

The gurdwara is open to visitors of all faiths. It offers free accommodation and meals (langar) to pilgrims. The best time to visit is during Guru Gobind Singh's birthday celebrations in December/January when special events are organized.
